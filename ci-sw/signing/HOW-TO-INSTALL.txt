Crisp for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel), VST3

1. Quit your DAW (FL Studio, Ableton Live ...).
2. Copy Crisp.vst3 into one of these folders, replacing any older Crisp.vst3 there:
     /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3          (for every user on this Mac)
     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3         (just for you)
   In Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the path, press Return.
3. Open your DAW and let it rescan its plugins. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.

This copy is signed by Sonic Weaponry and notarized by Apple, so you don't need to change any Gatekeeper or
security settings.
